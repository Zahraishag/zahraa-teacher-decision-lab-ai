ZAHRAA™ Teacher Decision Lab

Decision Before Generation

ZAHRAA™ Teacher Decision Lab helps teachers analyze complex instructional situations, compare pedagogically distinct alternatives, and approve a decision before an implementation plan is generated.

AI suggests. Teachers decide.

Why is it different?

Traditional AI toolsZAHRAA™ Teacher Decision Lab
Begin by generating contentBegin by analyzing the instructional situation
Often provide one immediate responsePresent multiple pedagogically distinct alternatives
Do not include an explicit decision-review stageRequire teacher approval before generating the plan
Focus on content productionFocus on the quality of the pedagogical decision

How it works

Analyze → Clarify → Compare → Approve → Generate

Designed for

  • Mixed-ability classrooms
  • Difficult or unsuccessful lessons
  • Lesson redesign decisions

Research Motivation

Recent advances in generative AI have made lesson planning faster than ever. This raises an open question: does faster content generation come at the cost of deliberate pedagogical reasoning? ZAHRAA™ Teacher Decision Lab explores this question by placing structured reasoning before content generation.

Core Contribution

ZAHRAA™ does not replace professional judgment. It makes the reasoning process visible, presents alternatives with their limitations, and keeps the teacher responsible for the final instructional decision.
