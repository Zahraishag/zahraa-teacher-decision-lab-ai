# Three-Minute Demo Script

Target length: **2:40–2:55**

## 0:00–0:18 — Problem

**Voiceover**

Most AI tools begin by generating a lesson plan. But the most important question comes first: what is the right pedagogical decision for this specific teaching situation?

**Screen**

Show the homepage hero and the decision diagram.

## 0:18–0:35 — Product promise

**Voiceover**

ZAHRAA™ Teacher Decision Lab introduces Decision Before Generation. AI proposes; the teacher decides.

**Screen**

Point to: situation, curriculum metadata, alternatives, teacher approval, implementation.

## 0:35–0:55 — DataHub role

**Voiceover**

DataHub provides the intended metadata and lineage layer. It organizes learning outcomes, teacher guidance, assessment policy, standards, grade level, and curriculum relationships so pedagogical evidence can be traced through the decision.

**Screen**

Scroll to the DataHub metadata and decision-lineage section.

**Transparency line**

This submission is a metadata-driven front-end prototype of the intended DataHub-powered workflow.

## 0:55–1:15 — Scenario

**Voiceover**

In this example, Grade 4 mathematics students can follow procedures but do not understand why the steps work.

**Screen**

Open the alternatives page and show the context panel.

## 1:15–1:48 — Alternatives

**Voiceover**

Instead of returning one answer, the system presents three pedagogically distinct alternatives: a visual conceptual approach, common-error analysis, and structured repartitioning. Each includes its focus, when it is useful, its limitation, and the curriculum evidence categories behind it.

**Screen**

Show all three cards, then open one analysis. Briefly point to “Why was this alternative suggested?”, evidence, rationale, and constraint.

## 1:48–2:10 — Human approval gate

**Voiceover**

The system cannot generate the plan yet. The teacher reviews the proposed decision, verifies its boundaries, and formally approves it.

**Screen**

Open teacher approval. Check the charter items, then approve.

## 2:10–2:36 — Plan generated from decision

**Voiceover**

Only after approval is the implementation plan generated. The learning objective, success criteria, lesson steps, differentiation, assessment, and exit card are derived from the approved pedagogical decision.

**Screen**

Show the implementation plan and Plan Provenance.

## 2:36–2:55 — Closing

**Voiceover**

This is not just lesson generation. It is a traceable educational decision workflow: situation, metadata, evidence, alternatives, teacher approval, and implementation. Decision Before Generation.

**Screen**

End on the completed Decision Trace and product name.
